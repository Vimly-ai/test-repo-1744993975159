# Expired Domain Hunting - Treasure Valley Businesses ($700K-$8M Revenue)

## STRATEGY OVERVIEW
Find domains from real businesses that closed or forgot to renew, buy them
at auction or after they drop, then offer to sell them back at a fair price.

## STEP 3: HOW TO FIND MORE TARGETS

### A. Monitor Idaho Secretary of State for Dissolved Businesses
1. Go to sosbiz.idaho.gov/search
2. Search by county (Ada County = Boise area, Canyon County = Nampa/Caldwell)
3. Filter by status: "Dissolved" or "Admin Dissolved"
4. Look for businesses dissolved in last 6-18 months
5. Google the business name to estimate revenue
6. Check if their domain is still registered or expiring

### B. Monitor BoiseDev Closures
1. Bookmark: boisedev.com/topics/development/closures/
2. Check weekly for new closure announcements
3. When a business announces closure, immediately look up their domain
4. Set a calendar reminder for 3, 6, 9, and 12 months after closure
   (that's when renewals typically lapse)

### C. Use ExpiredDomains.net Daily
1. Create a free account
2. Go to "Deleted Domains" section
3. Search these keywords daily:
   - boise, nampa, meridian, caldwell, eagle, kuna, idaho
   - treasure valley, gem state
   - Also search specific business names you're tracking
4. Filter by: .com TLD, Domain Age > 2 years, has backlinks
5. Set up keyword watchlists for automatic alerts

### D. Monitor GoDaddy Auctions
1. Get a membership ($4.99/yr)
2. Set keyword alerts for: boise, idaho, nampa, meridian
3. Check "Expiring" section daily
4. Bid on domains that match real businesses

### E. Use Wayback Machine to Verify
1. Before buying any domain, check web.archive.org
2. Enter the domain to see what the site used to look like
3. Verify it was a real business (not spam/parked)
4. Screenshot the old site - useful when approaching the owner

## STEP 5: WHAT MAKES A DOMAIN VALUABLE

When evaluating whether to buy an expired domain, check:

1. **Domain Age** - Older = more valuable (5+ years is great)
2. **Backlinks** - Check with Ahrefs or Moz. More quality backlinks = more value
3. **Brand Recognition** - Was it a well-known local business?
4. **Exact Match** - Does the domain match the business name exactly?
5. **Extension** - .com is king. .net/.org are worth less
6. **Search Rankings** - Did the old site rank well on Google?
7. **Traffic** - Check if domain still gets type-in traffic

### Tools to evaluate:
- **Ahrefs** (ahrefs.com) - Check backlinks and domain rating
- **Moz** (moz.com) - Domain Authority score
- **SEMrush** (semrush.com) - Traffic estimates
- **Web Archive** (web.archive.org) - See old site content

## IMPORTANT LEGAL REMINDERS

1. Only buy domains that are legitimately available (expired, auctioned, deleted)
2. Don't register domains of businesses that are still operating and have their domain active
3. Keep your resale prices reasonable (not 100x markup)
4. If a business has a registered trademark, they can file a UDRP to take the domain
5. Most small local businesses do NOT have registered trademarks - check USPTO.gov
6. Document everything - when you bought it, what you paid, auction receipts
7. Never use the domain to compete with or impersonate the business
